Wi-Fi HaLow OpenWrt Bridge - Change Log
========================================

1.2.0  2026-10-22
  Fix: uci-defaults-halow no longer disables every radioN at first boot.
       On a multi-radio device that removed the management radio.
  Fix: setup-relayd.sh SC2140 quoted assignment.
  Fix: lint.sh SC2181; exit codes checked directly with if ! cmd.
  Fix: lint.sh now includes install.sh in its target list.
  Fix: lint.sh non-zero exit gates the push; LINT_OVERRIDE=1 bypasses.
  Fix: start-ap.sh creates /var/run/hostapd before exec.
  Fix: install.sh creates /var/run/hostapd, copies all operator scripts,
       and documents the uci-defaults-halow opt-in.
  Fix: start-ap.sh country guard rewritten as if .. then .. fi.
  Docs: thermal warning hedged to datasheet verification instead of
        asserting 85 C.
  Add: .gitignore, CHANGELOG.txt.

1.1.0  2026-10-22

  Add: set-global-mac.sh explicit root check.
  Docs: GHSA/CVE-2026-7764 completeness.
  Docs: relayd caveat (not a full Layer-2 bridge).
  Docs: MM8108 thermal protection advisory.
